01 / Companion robot

A face.
A voice.
A physical response.

A hands on robotics project exploring interaction through voice assistance, expressive display output and movement.

The idea

Making interaction tangible.

My companion robot project explored how a robot could respond through more than a spoken answer. A face on a display and a moving head gave the interaction a physical and visual form.

The prototype brought together its enclosure, wheeled platform, display, electronics and actuation hardware.

My hands-on work

Connecting behaviour to hardware.

I worked and tested on voice assistance functionality, emoji expressions on an OLED display and head movement using a servo motor.

Working with a physical prototype helped me understand how packaging, wiring and software affect one another during integration.

Voice assistance Display interface Servo movement

Testing & research

From functional tests to a paper.

I checked the voice assistance response, the intended emoji display output and the servo driven head movement. These were functional prototype tests.

02 / NTTF Diploma Final Year Project

From a digital shape
to a part in my hand.

01 / Component design

This is a Painter"s Pallet component defined the geometry that the mould needed to produce.

Design / Manufacture / Moulded result

Following a design beyond the screen.

For my Diploma Final Year Project at NTTF, I worked on Producing an Injection Moulding Dieset to produce a Painter's Pallet component. The project Involved the Component Design, Core and Cavity Designing, Mould Designing and Production of Die, Quality Control and Taking Trails.

I created the component Geometry and the corresponding core and cavity. The part includes a central recessed area with NTTF lettering and circular recesses arranged around its perimeter.

My work also involved manufacture and Final assembly of the mould. Seeing the finished components come out of the Tool connected the CAD work with its practical purpose.

This project strengthened my understanding of the relationship between part geometry, tool construction and the moulded result. The final photograph shows components produced using the manufactured mould.

03 / SAE AeroTHON

Designed on screen.
Built into an aircraft.

UAV design and physical assembly for SAE AeroTHON, featuring a custom payload release mechanism.

Taking the design into the build.

For SAE AeroTHON, I worked on the drone CAD design and physical build, bringing together the aircraft structure, propulsion layout, flight electronics, landing gear and payload arrangement.

A key part of the work was the custom mechanism for retaining and releasing the payload.

Showing how it moves.

The CAD animation demonstrates the intended release sequence. A side mounted servo and linkage operate the retaining arrangement, allowing the lower panel to open and the payload to move out.

The aircraft photograph documents the physical build; the animation demonstrates the intended payload-release sequence in CAD.

04 / Ongoing Final Year Research Project

Seeing the scene.
Building the system.

VisionvGuided Autonomous Drone for Crowd Monitoring and Autonomous Navigation System.

Bringing the system together.

This is the physical drone platform I am using for my vision guided crowd monitoring research. I selected a ready made carbo fibre frame and am integrating the hardware and software around it.

My aim was to use a frame suited to supporting the propulsion system, flight electronics and vision hardware while I developed the perception and control integration.

I chose the frame with stiffness and durability in mind. My work focuses on person detection, ROS 2 communication, onboard processing and flight controller integration. Software functions have been tested; hardware integration and flight validation remain in progress.

Perception

From images to information.

Python, ROS 2 Jazzy, OpenCV and a fine tuned YOLOv8n person detector form the vision pipeline. ONNX inference runs on Raspberry Pi 4.

Software testing

Checking the chain.

I tested person counts, left/centre/right image-zone outputs and their connection to software decisions. MAVLink telemetry handling was tested in loopback.

Current stage

Integration continues.

Physical camera and Pixhawk integration, real telemetry checks and flight validation remain ongoing. Autonomous flight is not yet validated.

  1. Image input
  2. Person detection
  3. Crowd zones
  4. Decision & safety logic
  5. Flight-controller integration
